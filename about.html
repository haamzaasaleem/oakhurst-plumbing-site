Our story

Grown here, not shipped in

We started serving Oakhurst and the surrounding towns back in 1978, and the trade experience behind the company goes back to 1972. Over the years we've done every phase of plumbing in the mountains — the slab leaks under old cabins, the repipes on homes the winters have beaten up, and the septic systems that keep rural properties running.

The guy in the dive mask isn't just a logo. He's the promise: when the water's up to your knees and other outfits say it's too deep, that's exactly when we show up. No problem too deep.
